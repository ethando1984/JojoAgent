# Privacy Policy: Jojo Alexa skill

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026

This policy applies to the **Jojo** skill for Amazon Alexa (the "Skill"), provided by **AitechAsia** ("we", "us"). It explains what information the Skill handles when you talk to it. It does not cover the Jojo desktop app, which has its own documentation in this repository.

## What the Skill receives

When you use the Skill, Alexa converts your speech to text and sends us:

- the **text of what you asked** (we do not receive your voice recording);
- standard request details from Alexa, such as the locale and a session identifier.

The Skill does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, address, payment details, or location, and it does not use Alexa account linking or Alexa permissions.

## How we use it

We send the text of your question, together with up to your last three questions and answers in the same conversation, to the AitechAsia Hub (`hub.aitech.asia`). The Hub generates an answer using an AI model, and the Skill speaks that answer back to you. We use this information only to answer you and to run, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

## What is stored

- **Conversation context** is kept by Alexa for the length of the session only. The Skill does not store it in its own database.
- **Service logs**: the Skill's own logs record error types (for example a timeout), not the text of your questions.
- **The Hub** may keep usage and audit records of requests it handles, such as time, model used, and request size, to operate, bill, and secure the service. Because the Skill uses a shared service account, these records are not tied to your Amazon account.

## Sharing

The text of your question is processed by the AitechAsia Hub and by the AI model providers the Hub routes it to in order to produce an answer. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Amazon processes your voice and Alexa data under [Amazon's own privacy notice](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ).

## Your choices

Do not say personal or sensitive information to the Skill. You can end a conversation at any time by saying "stop", and you can disable the Skill in the Alexa app (More > Skills & Games > Your Skills). You can review or delete your Alexa voice history in the Alexa app under Settings > Alexa Privacy.

To ask about, or request deletion of, any information we hold about your use of the Skill, open an issue at <https://github.com/ethando1984/JojoAgent/issues>. Please do not put personal information in a public issue; say you have a privacy request and we will arrange a private way to follow up.

## Children

The Skill is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and the current text is always at <https://github.com/ethando1984/JojoAgent/blob/main/docs/privacy-policy.md>.
